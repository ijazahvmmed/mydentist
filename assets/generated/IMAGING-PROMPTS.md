Created 2026-10-16 using the built-in imagegen tool. Generic educational artwork, not patient radiographs, diagnostic images or clinic equipment photographs. Sources: opg-panoramic.png and cbct-volume.png in this directory; production AVIF/WebP derivatives in public/images/technology.

## OPG final prompt

Use case: scientific-educational. Create an original generic illustrative panoramic dental imaging asset for a premium dental website, not a real patient radiograph. Wide horizontal 2:1 composition, landscape. A convincing orthopantomogram (OPG) style view showing the complete upper AND lower adult dental arches, incisors, canines, premolars, molars, clearly distinguishable tooth roots within alveolar bone, the broad mandibular jaw with its ascending rami and condyles on both sides, and upper jaw/sinus structure. A near-frontal flattened panoramic projection, not a perspective render of floating crowns, not an oval, not a starburst. Dentition should have plausible natural proportions and even arrangement, root anatomy readable, fine trabecular bone texture, anatomically coherent jaw structure. Full anatomy centered with generous dark margin, no cropping of condyles or mandibular border. Black/navy background #071a27, soft blue-white radiographic grayscale rendering, fine detail and tonal depth, restrained luminosity, no oversaturated neon. Healthy generic anatomy, no pathology, disease markers, diagnostic annotations, circles, labels, patient information, text, UI, logos or watermarks. Elegantly clear radiograph-inspired educational illustration with precision and natural texture. It must immediately read as full-mouth panoramic dental imaging. The image will receive a subtle scan-band animation in CSS; do not bake a scan band into the image.

## CBCT final prompt

Use case: scientific-educational. Create an original premium medical educational illustration to explain dental CBCT, cone beam computed tomography. Landscape 4:3 composition. Show a coherent anatomically plausible lower jaw and upper dental arch with full tooth crowns and roots inside semitransparent jaw bone, in a restrained three-quarter volumetric view. A softly bounded transparent scan volume surrounds the jaw, with just two delicate intersecting sectional planes (one axial and one sagittal) passing through it to suggest a reconstructed three-dimensional dental image. The bone has realistic fine internal structure, blue-white teeth are distinct and naturally proportioned, faint translucent bone softly reveals roots. Keep it a medical volume visualization, not an array of floating teeth, not clinical machinery, not a patient scan. Place the whole jaw within a generous open margin. Background deep muted teal/navy #102c3c, pale blue and soft white material tones, scientific editorial quality, understated light and beautiful tonal depth. No bright neon, no cyberpunk, no hologram beams, no rings, no grids of software, no fake measurements, no text, no labels, no logos, no watermarks, no pathology or diagnostic circles. Balanced and professional image designed for a dental clinic website explaining 3D imaging.
